# General maintenance-review guidance

This is FleetMind's general review guidance. For equipment-specific procedures, operating limits, and safety requirements, follow approved site procedures and the applicable manufacturer documentation.

## Triage and confirmation

For a high-priority alert, bring the recorded reading and alert context to a qualified maintenance reviewer using the site's escalation process. For a review alert, check the provenance, units, sensor plausibility, and recent operating changes. Where available, compare with verified readings and maintenance records for the same asset.

## Review evidence

A no-alert assessment does not replace scheduled inspections, operator observations, or independent safety checks. Flag gaps in the recorded evidence explicitly. Record human confirmation and the evidence used before reaching a diagnosis or authorizing maintenance. FleetMind scores and feature attributions alone cannot establish a fault or authorize work.
